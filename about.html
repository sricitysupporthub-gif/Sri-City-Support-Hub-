About Sri City Support Hub

Sri City Support Hub is a community-driven initiative dedicated to connecting, supporting, and empowering workers, job seekers, and residents living and working in Sri City, Andhra Pradesh.

Our Mission

We aim to bridge the gap between job opportunities and skilled talent while providing essential local resources—ranging from reliable room rentals and transport information to community assistance desks.

Founder & Community Desk

Created to serve the industrial workforce and local community with verified, transparent, and direct support.
