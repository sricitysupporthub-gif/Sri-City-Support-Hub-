About Founder & Mission

Empowering workers, job seekers, and residents with dignity and verified guidance.

VY

Vishnu Yadav

Founder, Sri City Support Hub

"Sri City aane wale har worker aur youth ke samne authentic jobs aur rehne-khane ki sabse badi samasya hoti hai. Aksar log middle-men ke chakkar me padkar apna paisa gawa dete hain. Maine **Sri City Support Hub** ki shuruat isi gap ko bharne ke liye ki hai—ek aisa community platform jo bina kisi fees ke samman ke sath real help provide kare."

Our Long-Term Vision

We are building Sri City Support Hub step-by-step to become an indispensable regional community utility, scaling towards nationwide industrial corridors.

  • 100% Genuine, verified vacancies without middle-man fees.
  • Comprehensive directory for budget PG, mess, and daily transport in Tada and Sri City.
  • Multilingual expansion supporting Telugu, Tamil, Hindi, and English candidates.
