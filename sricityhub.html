04 • How the Hub works

Check information yourself first

The website is designed so visitors can spend time exploring details instead of having to contact the team for every basic question.

1
Search

Search the company, role, service or local category you need.

2
Open Details

Read qualification, salary range, role, documents and available information.

3
Check Location

Use the map and route buttons to independently understand the area and visit location.

4
Take Action

Apply, email, WhatsApp the relevant person, or use the final support option if needed.
