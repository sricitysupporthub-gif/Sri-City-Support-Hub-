Private Industrial & Facility Jobs

Role examples help you search by industry. They are not confirmed live vacancies unless a listing clearly says so. Confirm salary, shift, age limit and hiring status with the employer.

Important: Salary figures are indicative examples, not offers or guaranteed take-home pay. Many roles may ask for 18–45 years; some employers accept candidates up to 50–55 based on work, fitness and policy. Age and gender eligibility are employer-specific. Some roles may accept women and candidates up to 45–55 years depending on work type, fitness and company policy; this is not guaranteed for every role. Check the actual age limit, shift, OT and in-hand salary with the employer. Never pay an unofficial job fee.

Job safety & reporting

Report an Incorrect or Suspicious Job Listing

If a vacancy has incorrect salary, fake interview details, demands money, or uses a misleading company name, please share the listing and available proof. The Hub will review the information; do not send passwords, OTPs or sensitive identity documents.
